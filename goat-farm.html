Modern Livestock Management

Elevated Slatted Flooring & Stall-Fed Architecture

Our shed in Pilol, Vadodara, is built on an elevated wooden/plastic slatted system. Droppings and urine separate immediately, keeping goats 100% dry, eliminating foot rot, pneumonia, and parasite transmission.

Slatted Flooring

Raised 3 feet above ground for complete ventilation and zero dampness.

Automated Drinkers

Nutritional Silage Feed

Strict Medical Protocol

Regular deworming every 3 months, PPR, ET, and FMD vaccines administered on time.

Nursery Care Protocol

Bakra, Bakri & Kids (बकरा, बकरी और बच्चे)

A goat farm's future is determined by how it nurtures its newborn kids. At Gujarat Agro Farm, we provide structured neonatal care to guarantee over 95% kid survival rates.

Our 4-Stage Kid Management Program:

  • Colostrum within 1 Hour: Feeding mother's first golden milk rich in maternal antibodies within 60 minutes of birth.
  • Navel Cord Sterilization: Iodine dipping of the umbilical cord immediately to prevent joint-ill and naval sepsis.
  • Starter Creep Feed at 14 Days: Introducing micro-pellet creep feed with 20% protein to initiate early rumen development.
  • Gradual Weaning at 90 Days: Smooth transition to green fodder and silage without growth stunting.

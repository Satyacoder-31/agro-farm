Scientific & Traditional Fusion

Biosecure, Free-Range & Climate-Controlled Brooding

At Gujarat Agro Farm, we believe quality poultry starts with pure genetics and strict environmental biosecurity. Our birds enjoy spacious open-air runs, high-protein organic grain diets, and zero prophylactic antibiotics.

Marek's & Lasota Done

All day-old chicks vaccinated right at hatch to eliminate early mortality.

Open Pasture Grazing

Birds forage on clover grass, natural worms, and mineral grit for rich golden yolks.

Climate Brooding

Precise 33°C to 35°C infrared ceramic heat control during the fragile first 14 days.

All-India Safe Transit

Specialized ventilated chick boxes with hydration gel for overnight train or bus delivery.

The golden standard of rural poultry farming. Fast 90-day growth to 1.4kg - 1.6kg live weight with desi meat texture, plus 180-200 tinted brown eggs/year.

Weight: 1.5kg in 90 Days Eggs: 190/yr High Immunity

Pure Kadaknath (Kali Masi)

कड़कनाथ मुर्गी मुर्गा (100% Jet Black)

Famous medicinal bird with black plumage, skin, meat, and bones. Contains 25%+ protein with under 1% cholesterol, high iron, and immense medicinal appeal.

Black Meat & Bone Protein: 25.4% High Market Price

Broiler Turkey (टर्की)

બ્રોઇલર ટર્કી (Broad Breasted)

Massive game birds reaching 8kg to 14kg in 5 to 6 months. Unsurpassed lean meat dressing percentage, prized by star hotels and health-conscious meat lovers.

Live Wt: 8-14 kg Low Fat Meat Festive Demand

Guinea Fowl (तीतर / ચકોર)

गिनी फाउल (Hardiest Farm Bird)

Remarkably hardy birds requiring minimal shelter. Naturally pest-control orchards by eating ticks and beetles, yielding game-flavored delicious desi meat.

Zero Disease Risk Thick Shelled Eggs Farm Guard Bird

Black Australorp

બ્લેક ઓસ્ટ્રેલોર્પ (Heavy Layer)

World record holder laying 300+ large brown eggs annually. Docile temperament, beautiful beetle-green iridescent sheen, and heavy dual-purpose meat body.

Eggs: 280-320/yr Docile & Calm Weight: 2.5-3.5kg

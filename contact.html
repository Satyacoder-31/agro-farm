Visitor Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions for Farm Visitors

Everything you need to know before visiting Gujarat Agro Farm.

Is prior appointment necessary to visit Gujarat Agro Farm?
While walk-in visitors are warmly welcomed during operational hours (8:00 AM to 7:00 PM), scheduling your visit in advance via WhatsApp or phone ensures our senior breed manager is personally present to walk you through the pens and answer technical questions.
Can I purchase and carry livestock / chicks in my private vehicle?
Yes! We provide certified transport cartons for chicks and poultry birds, and assist with safe tethering and ventilation for goats. We also supply health vaccination cards and transit NOC papers where required.
What biosecurity protocols should visitors observe?
To safeguard our flock, all visitors must step through our gentle potassium permanganate disinfectant footbaths at the shed entrances. Hand sanitizers are placed throughout the nursery areas.

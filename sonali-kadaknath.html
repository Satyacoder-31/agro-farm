Sonali Murgi & Murga (सोनाली मुर्गी मुर्गा)

The Sonali chicken is the undisputed champion of commercial desi poultry farming. Originally bred as a cross between Rhode Island Red (RIR) males and Fayoumi females, Sonali carries the authentic visual appearance and firm, tasty texture of desi chicken, but grows at more than twice the speed.

Male (Murga) vs. Female (Murgi) Traits:

  • Sonali Murga (Male): Striking fiery golden-copper plumage, upright bright-red single comb, and strong yellow shanks. Reaches 1.5kg - 1.8kg in 90 to 100 days with remarkable feed conversion.
  • Sonali Murgi (Female): Buff-golden speckles, compact body, and begins laying rich tinted brown eggs at just 19 to 20 weeks of age, sustaining 180-200 eggs annually.

Pure Kadaknath (Kali Masi / કડકનાથ)

The world-famous jet black chicken originating from Jhabua and Dhar districts of Madhya Pradesh. Kadaknath has high melanin deposition (fibromelanosis) which turns not just its feathers, but its skin, beak, tongue, comb, meat, and internal bones completely black.

Why Health Specialists Recommend Kadaknath:

  • 25.4% Protein: Highest among all chicken breeds (ordinary broilers only have 18%).
  • Low Cholesterol (< 1%): Compared to 13-25% in regular poultry meat, making it ideal for cardiovascular and diabetic patients.
  • Rich in Iron & Amino Acids: High haemoglobin boosting qualities and essential linoleic acid.
  • Medicinal Homoeopathy Base: Widely utilized in traditional Ayurvedic and tribal wellness therapies.

Our Traditional Organic Farming Philosophy

In strict adherence to traditional Indian natural farming (પ્રાકૃતિક ખેતી), we regenerate our soil biology rather than feeding plants with petroleum-derived salts.

Natural fermented bio-fertilizers and cold-pressed neem seed decoctions for organic pest repulsion.

Water-saving drip lines feeding root zones precisely, conserving 65% groundwater.

Major Freshwater Species We Cultivate

From Indian Major Carps (IMC) to high-protein Tilapia, available live from our pond straight to your transport containers.

Most Popular

Rohu (રોહુ / रोहू)

Labeo Rohita

The king of Indian carps. Sweet white meat, tender flakes, and firm texture. Reaches 1.2kg to 2.5kg market size with high consumer demand across Gujarat.

Fast Growing

Katla (કટલા / कतला)

Gibelion Catla

Broad-headed surface feeder characterized by rapid growth. Ideal for ceremonial feasts and large family banquets, reaching 2.0kg to 4.0kg.

Year-Round Harvest

GIFT Tilapia (તિલાપિયા)

Oreochromis Niloticus

Genetically improved farmed tilapia with high meat-to-bone ratio and boneless fillet suitability. 100% odorless and harvested fresh every morning.

Zero Waste Ecosystem

How Fish Farming Powers Our Vegetable Orchards

The natural nitrogen-rich effluent from our fish ponds is piped directly into our papaya, lemon, and tomato orchards via drip irrigation, eliminating chemical fertilizers.

Biofloc Microbial Protein

Beneficial bacteria convert toxic ammonia into high-protein flocs that fish graze continuously.

Nutrient Water Recirculation

Rich in nitrates and phosphorus, boosting crop yields naturally by 35%.

Live Catch Guarantee

Loaded live into oxygenated transport tanks to ensure 100% freshness at your retail counter.

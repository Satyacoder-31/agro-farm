African Boer (અફ્રિકન બોઅર બકરાં)

Originally developed in South Africa, the Boer goat is universally acknowledged as the finest meat goat on earth. They possess heavy muscling, rapid growth, and outstanding carcass traits with an impressive dressing percentage between 58% and 62%.

Key Characteristics:

  • Appearance: Signature reddish-chestnut head and neck, white blaze on forehead, curved backward horns, and deep, broad chest.
  • Stud Purpose: A single purebred Boer buck crossbred with local desi or Sirohi does doubles the kid growth rate from generation F1.
  • Weight: Adult Bucks: 90kg - 120kg | Adult Does: 65kg - 85kg.

Sirohi Breed (સિરહી બકરા-બકરી)

Hailing from southern Rajasthan and northern Gujarat, Sirohi is the most popular commercial breed in India. Extremely resistant to extreme temperatures, drought, and rough fodder grazing.

Key Characteristics:

  • Appearance: Cylindrical compact body, brown coat with circular dark brown or white patches, drooping leaf-like flat ears, and small curved horns.
  • Twinning Ability: Over 60% of does give birth to twins after the first kidding.
  • Weight: Adult Bucks: 55kg - 75kg | Adult Does: 40kg - 55kg.

Jamnapari (જમનાપારી બકરાં)

The majestic aristocrat of Indian goats, hailing from the ravines of the Chambal river in Uttar Pradesh. Famous for its regal stature, long drooping pendulous ears reaching 28-30 cm, and distinctive Roman parrot-mouth nose.

Key Characteristics:

  • Appearance: Predominantly white body, extremely long pendulous ears, Roman arched nose, and thick luxurious feathers of hair on hind legs.
  • High Dairy Yield: Produces 2.5 to 3.5 liters of sweet, easily digestible milk with 5% butterfat daily.
  • Weight: Adult Bucks: 65kg - 90kg | Adult Does: 45kg - 60kg.

Barbari (બરબરી બકરા-બકરી)

The deer-like, elegant Barbari goat is the undisputed favorite for urban stall-feeding and backyard hobby farming. Compact in size, highly docile, and prolific breeders that reproduce twice in 14 months.

Key Characteristics:

  • Appearance: Small compact body with short erect prick ears pointed upwards, white coat with dark brown/tan spots, and alert eyes.
  • Prolific Reproduction: Regularly produces twins and triplets; early sexual maturity at 7 to 8 months.
  • Weight: Adult Bucks: 35kg - 48kg | Adult Does: 25kg - 38kg.

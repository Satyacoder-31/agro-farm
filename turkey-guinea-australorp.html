Pearl Guinea Fowl (गिनी फाउल / तीतर-चकोर)

Frequently Asked Questions

Common Queries Regarding Specialty Birds

Everything you need to know before ordering Turkeys, Guinea Fowls, and Black Australorps from Gujarat Agro Farm.

Can Turkeys and Guinea Fowl be raised together on an open farm?
Yes, absolutely! Both species thrive exceptionally well under free-range and semi-intensive conditions. Guinea Fowls act as watchful farm alarms that deter predators and snakes, while Turkeys graze peacefully alongside them without aggression.
What is the minimum order quantity for day-old chicks / keets?
For local pickup from our Pilol (Vadodara) farm, there is no minimum quantity! For train or bus transit across Gujarat, Maharashtra, MP, or Rajasthan, the minimum box order is 50 to 100 chicks to ensure adequate mutual warmth during transport.
Do Black Australorp hens go broody or require an incubator?
Black Australorps have had broodiness bred out of them to maximize continuous egg production throughout all 12 months. For hatching chicks, we recommend using a simple incubator or placing fertile eggs under a broody desi hen.
